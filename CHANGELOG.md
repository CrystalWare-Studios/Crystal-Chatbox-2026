# Changelog

## v6.0.0

### Patreon support
- Connect your Patreon account to your Crystal account from the account menu
- Donor leaderboard, ranked by lifetime support
- Patreon Perks tab: custom accent color, custom background image
- Supporter-only leaderboard name color
- Supporter-only leaderboard banner color (tints your whole row, not just your name)
- Refresh Patreon status button, so you don't have to wait for the next renewal
- Connecting now pulls in a pledge you already had, instead of showing $0 until next month
- Patreon link in the app and on the website

### Website
- New Leaderboards page at crystalwarestudios.com/leaderboards, live from the app
- Supporter name and banner colors show on the website too
- Support and Leaderboards links added to every page
- Fixed the nav running off the side of the screen on phones

### Chatbox
- 24-hour clock option (Integrations, Weather & Time Zone)
- 5 new frames: Rounded, Double Line, Heavy, Curly Braces, Corner Accent
- Fixed frame borders not lining up with the text inside them
- Fixed multi-character frame edges being the wrong width
- Fixed the slim chatbox suffix getting added twice
- Fixed long lyrics getting cut off in scroll mode

### Music
- Switching music source is now instant (was up to 5 seconds)
- Switching source no longer carries over the old source's connection backoff or error count
- Turning lyrics on now takes effect immediately instead of a few seconds later

### Fixes
- Fixed reaction rules sometimes disappearing right after you added one
- Fixed custom colors and background never actually being applied
- Fixed a settings save that could drop data instead of saving it
